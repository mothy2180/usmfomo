# USM permission letter (Student Discipline Rules 2024, r.12)

Rule 12 of the USM Student Discipline Rules 2024 (P.U.(A) 329) requires the
Vice-Chancellor's prior written permission before a student publishes an
electronic "document". usmfomo is a public website, so ask before launch. This
has the longest lead time of anything in the project: send it in week 1.

Below are two ready-to-send versions of the same letter, **Bahasa Malaysia**
first (the version to sign and send) and **English** (as an attachment or for an
English-speaking reader). The request for `usmfomo.cs.usm.my` (paragraph 7) is
optional; delete it if you do not want to ask.

## Before sending

- [ ] Fill in every `[…]`: your name, matric number, programme, school, contact
      details, the date, and the names/titles of the Vice-Chancellor and the Dean.
- [ ] Check the citation against the copy of the rules HEPA gives you (exact
      title, gazette number and rule number), and adjust paragraph 2 if it differs.
- [ ] Send it **through** your School (Dean's office) and HEPA, as addressed;
      ask each office how they want it (signed PDF by email, or on paper).
- [ ] Attach: A, screenshots of the landing page, the dashboard and an event
      page showing the disclaimer; B, a link to the source code
      (<https://github.com/mothy2180/usmfomo>).
- [ ] Keep a signed copy. Record the date sent, and later the reply, in
      `docs/PROJECT.md` (§7 Open items). Follow up after two weeks.
- [ ] Until written permission arrives, launch only if you accept the risk, and
      write that decision down in the decision log.

3. **Tujuan.** Pelajar USM belum mempunyai satu tempat untuk mengetahui
aktiviti yang sedang dan akan berlangsung di kampus, sehingga banyak program
kelab dan pusat pengajian terlepas pandang. usmfomo memaparkan acara akan datang

Pelajar boleh melayarinya tanpa mendaftar atau log masuk. Projek ini bukan untuk
keuntungan: tiada iklan dan tiada bayaran.

4. **Data yang dikendalikan.**
   a) Pelajar yang melayari laman tidak perlu memberikan sebarang data peribadi.
      Tiada akaun pelajar, tiada kuki pengiklanan dan tiada alat analitik atau
      penjejakan pihak ketiga.
   b) Hanya kelab dan pusat pengajian yang telah disahkan melalui saluran rasmi

      saya selaku pentadbir. Log masuk menggunakan nama pengguna; tiada e-mel
      dikumpul atau dihantar.
   c) Kandungan yang disimpan ialah butiran acara yang diterbitkan oleh
      penganjur sendiri: tajuk, tempat, masa mula dan tamat, keterangan ringkas,
      pautan pendaftaran dan poster. Poster diproses dalam pelayar dan data
      lokasinya (EXIF/GPS) dibuang sebelum dimuat naik.
   d) Setiap hantaran dan posternya dipadam secara automatik selepas acara
      tamat. Log audit perubahan (siapa mengubah apa dan bila) disimpan selama
      180 hari bagi tujuan keselamatan.
   e) Laman ini dihoskan pada pelan percuma Cloudflare Pages dan Supabase
      (pusat data di Singapura). Kod sumbernya terbuka kepada umum (lesen
      AGPL-3.0) supaya boleh disemak oleh sesiapa sahaja.

5. **Kawalan dan moderasi.**
   a) Hanya akaun kelab dan pusat pengajian yang disahkan boleh menerbitkan.
      Log masuk dilindungi CAPTCHA dan boleh menggunakan pengesahan dua faktor;
      had bilangan hantaran dan saiz fail dikuatkuasakan oleh pangkalan data.
   b) Selaku pentadbir, saya boleh menyembunyikan atau memadam mana-mana
      hantaran serta-merta, menggantung akaun, dan menghentikan semua penerbitan
      dengan satu suis.
   c) Setiap halaman mempunyai pautan untuk melaporkan masalah kepada pentadbir,
      dan sebuah halaman peraturan menerangkan kandungan yang dibenarkan.
   d) Saya akan menurunkan mana-mana kandungan, atau keseluruhan laman,
      serta-merta atas arahan Universiti.

2. I, [full name], a [programme] student at the [School], hereby apply for your
written permission under rule 12 of the Universiti Sains Malaysia (Student
Discipline) Rules 2024 [P.U.(A) 329] to publish a website named **usmfomo** at
<https://usmfomo.pages.dev>.

3. **Purpose.** USM students have no single place to see what is happening on
campus, so many club and school events go unnoticed. usmfomo lists upcoming
events published by clubs, societies and schools in one place. Students can
browse without registering or signing in. The project is non-commercial: no
advertising and no fees.

4. **Data handled.**
   a) Students who browse the site give no personal data. There are no student
      accounts, no advertising cookies and no third-party analytics or tracking.
   b) Only clubs and schools verified through their official channels receive
      one posting account each, created by me as the administrator. Logins use
      usernames; no email address is collected and no email is sent.
   c) The content stored is the event details the organisers publish
      themselves: title, venue, start and end time, a short description, a
      registration link and a poster. Posters are processed in the browser and
      their location data (EXIF/GPS) is removed before upload.
   d) Each post and its poster are deleted automatically after the event ends.
      An audit log of changes (who changed what, and when) is kept for 180 days
      for security purposes.
   e) The site is hosted on the free plans of Cloudflare Pages and Supabase
      (data centre in Singapore). Its source code is public (AGPL-3.0 licence),
      so anyone can review it.

5. **Controls and moderation.**
   a) Only verified club and school accounts can publish. Logins are protected
      by a CAPTCHA and can use two-factor authentication; limits on the number
      of posts and on file sizes are enforced by the database.
   b) As administrator I can hide or delete any post immediately, suspend
      accounts, and stop all publishing with a single switch.
   c) Every page links to a way of reporting a problem to the administrator, and
      a rules page explains what may be posted.
   d) I will take down any content, or the whole site, immediately at the
      University's request.

6. **Disclaimer.** The site does not use the USM logo, crest or corporate
colours. Every page shows the following disclaimer in Bahasa Malaysia and
English:

   > usmfomo is an independent, unofficial student project, not affiliated
   > with or endorsed by Universiti Sains Malaysia. Events are posted by the
   > clubs and schools themselves.

   I am also prepared to rename the site if the University considers the name
   "usmfomo" unsuitable.

7. **Optional request.** If permitted, I would also like to request that the
manager of the cs.usm.my domain (School of Computer Sciences) create the
subdomain **usmfomo.cs.usm.my** as a CNAME record pointing to
`usmfomo.pages.dev`. This does not affect the main request in paragraph 2.

8. I will comply with any conditions the University sets, and I am glad to give
a further explanation or a demonstration at any convenient time. Enclosed are
